Ambient hero video (optional)
==============================
Place a short, muted looping clip here as:

  ambient.mp4

Tips for a professional academic look:
- 8–20 seconds, seamless loop
- Soft lab / abstract / particle footage (no face cam)
- 720p is enough; keep file under ~3–5 MB
- Desaturated or low-contrast works best under the text overlay

The site auto-detects this file. If missing, only the art/gradient layer shows.
Disable motion: OS "Reduce motion" hides video automatically.
